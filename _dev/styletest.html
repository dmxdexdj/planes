Where to use this background

The rule for the whole site.

This background is the main background for every non-hub page

A hub page is a page that leads to other pages, like index.html. Hubs get the full hero: the long sunset gradient, drifting clouds, parallax, and the big title.

Every other page (like planecards.html and this style test) uses the background behind this page: the fixed one-screen sunset with stars, the sun tucked behind the smooth hills, and the hills fading out at the bottom. It stays put while the content scrolls over it.
